Fun Facts

Music & Records The Members ARMY & Achievements
BTS debuted on June 13, 2013. There are seven members in BTS. BTS's fandom is called ARMY.
“Dynamite” was their first song performed entirely in English. RM is the group's leader. Their official group colors are often associated with purple.
“Spring Day” was released in 2017. Jin is the oldest member. “Butter” earned BTS multiple Billboard Hot 100 No. 1 debuts.
BTS explores hip-hop, pop, R&B, and rock. Jung Kook is the youngest member. BTS performed at the United Nations General Assembly.
“Love Yourself” is one of their major album series. Several members release solo music. Fans often use the phrase “I purple you.”
