Members

The seven members of BTS:

RM

The leader and main rapper of the group

Jin

The oldest member and a main vocalist

Suga

A lead rapper and main music producer

J-Hope

A main dancer, rapper, and sub-vocalist.

Jimin

A main dancer and lead vocalist

V

A vocalist and dancer

Jungkook

The youngest member, main vocalist, and lead dancer.

One interesting detail: BTS stands for Bangtan Sonyeondan, which translates to "Bulletproof Boy Scouts".
