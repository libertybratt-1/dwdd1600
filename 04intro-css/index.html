Who is BTS?

BTS is a South Korean boy band that became one of the biggest music groups in the world.

BTS stands for Bangtan Sonyeondan (방탄소년단), which roughly translates to “Bulletproof Boy Scouts.” They also use the English meaning “Beyond the Scene.”

The group debuted in 2013 under Big Hit Entertainment (now part of HYBE).

Source for this text.

Themes

BTS started with a more hip-hop-focused sound and gradually expanded into pop, R&B, rock, and other styles. A huge part of their appeal is that their music often deals with growing up, insecurity, mental health, relationships, ambition, identity, and self-worth.
